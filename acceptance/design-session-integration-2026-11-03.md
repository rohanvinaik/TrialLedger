  Scope/profile/resource/unknown blockers still gate the reduction. Raw blockers and actual proofs survive.

The source and reporting paths stay independent. General prose compilation and complete protocol writing
remain unfinished. Legacy standalone requests retain their documented caller-declaration contract; managed
