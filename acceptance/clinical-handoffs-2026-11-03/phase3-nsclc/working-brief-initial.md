# Initial simulated design brief

This is a simulated design exercise for an adults-only NSCLC study after at least one advanced-disease line. The choices below are invented for the exercise; historical source facts and possible readings are tracked separately.

1. **Which setting are we choosing?** The draft currently allows the finite pair previously-treated/maintenance. Merely adding the words “previously treated” did not encode a restriction: the input was accepted but had no registered consumer. At least one prior line does not itself mean progression or distinguish maintenance from another later treatment. An explicit separate finite setting restriction was therefore supplied for the simulated draft. Both values remain in that declared coordinate; this is not proof of two complete clinically feasible protocols.
2. **Does the China study's entry ECOG criterion govern crossover?** Two source-anchored proposed readings were kept: initial enrollment only, or hypothetically requiring the same limit again at crossover. For ECOG 2 at crossover, the first reading does not address that treatment phase; the second would exclude it if that reading were right. The combined answer remains open because the readings apply the restriction differently. No source reading was confirmed or adopted into the new study.
3. **Adults only?** The simulated new draft explicitly requires age 18 Years or older. Separately, the China record's structured minimum age mechanically supports that restriction for its own registry-general eligibility. The radiotherapy record supplies no numerical minimum: its missing age is held, not copied from the other study.

The treatment arms/regimen, exact advanced-line definition, progression requirement, biomarker/histology policy, study population, primary estimand, visit schedule/windows, crossover policy, safety/stopping rules, analysis methods, power/success criteria and complete protocol remain open. The saved source records provide documentary context; they do not choose these parameters for the new trial.
