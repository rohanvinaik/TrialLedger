## Decision list

1. **One-day functional assessment or prospective cough follow-up? — Open clinical choice.** NCT07119099 is cross-sectional,21 actual enrollment, with functional/exercise tests and other measures registered atDay 1. It selects IPF with unchanged treatment and stable clinical status over 3 months. NCT02009293 is a prospective cohort,43 actual enrollment, selecting IPF with daily cough and patients about to start pirfenidone in routine practice; its description records cough monitoring before treatment and at 4 and 12 weeks. Shared IPF wording does not establish equivalent populations, treatment assignment, or outcome definitions. Next action: choose the new cohort's clinical purpose. Do not claim this observational study randomized pirfenidone or that a registered time frame is an observed visit.

2. **Which age range? — Structured facts and an unresolved prose boundary.** NCT07119099 reports structured 18–85, while its eligibility prose says over 18. NCT02009293 reports 40–85. The source-age operation retains each exact study/snapshot and compiles only the structured general-age rule. Age 30 is allowed but not required by the first range and ruled out by the second. The strictly-over 18 proposed reading is compatible with the structured floor and conditionally rules out exactly 18; structured data alone allow 18 but do not require everyone to be 18. This is a boundary interpretation needing the underlying protocol, not a formal contradiction or a reason to erase either source channel.

   For the new draft, the explicitly simulated baseline age range 18–85 is revised to 40–85. Actual managed reasoning changes age 30 from allowed/notrequired to ruled out, decided by draft-age. Age 50 remains allowed/notrequired in both. These results check represented age restrictions alone, not all clinical eligibility. The two historical source-age analyses remain unchanged. Next action: decide whether the new cohort should have a 40-year floor and explicitly settle inclusivity, rather than importing a comparator's whole population silently.

3. **From enrollment or first treatment dose? — Simulated design choice with a strict consequence.** The source wording puts cough recording before pirfenidone initiation and repeats it during treatment; treating that as a specific new-study clock is a choice. In the invented draft only, first dose is permitted 0–2 weeks after enrollment. Baseline attaches primary follow-up to enrollment+12 weeks; the revision attaches it to first-dose+12 weeks. The temporal solver computes enrollment+12–14 weeks in the revised design. This 0–2 week allowance is invented, not reported by either study. Actual-primary remains unconnected, so no actual assessment time or allowed visit window is computed. Next action: specify the origin, allowed window, baseline recording duration and policy for delayed/nonstarted treatment. Do not use a 12-week follow-up label to certify 12 weeks of exposure or complete collection.

4. **What do we mean by stable treatment and permitted concomitant medication? — Source wording retained, mostly unencoded.** NCT07119099 requires unchanged treatment/stable clinical status 3 months. NCT02009293 has separate cough duration, VAS, FVC/TLCOc and medication/infection restrictions, including multiple 2/4/6 week clocks and prednisone-equivalent wording. The program has not interpreted these passages, equated months to weeks, converted drug classes, or established that requiring a stable treatment means every participant must use one. Next action: encode material alternatives and conditional medication rules with explicit populations and time anchors after clinical clarification.

5. **Can this evidence set the estimand, power or effect target? — Not resolved here.** Registered endpoints describe plans; no results are posted. Treatment is according to normal practice/physician discretion in the cough cohort. No random assignment, causal treatment effect, estimand strategy, statistical model, effect size, sample size or efficacy conclusion was computed. Next action: design these elements explicitly and obtain the relevant protocol/analysis sources; do not replace absent outcome observations with the source's hypothesis.
