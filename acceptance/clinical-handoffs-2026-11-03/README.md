## What these conversations establish

- Identified: source-bound age facts remain usable while another scope or clock is unresolved; explicit
  draft choices change their own consequences; effect/contrast explanations reach the clinical handoff.
- Abstained: unresolved source readings, population equivalence, scale equivalence and unconnected actual
  events remain open. Exact source location does not establish a normalized clinical meaning.
- Wrong then repaired: retained operator request-shape/query-vocabulary mistakes were refused; separately
  corrected requests were run. These are not hidden or counted as source findings.
- Not tested or not built: complete clinical eligibility, full escalation/stopping procedures, statistical
  operating characteristics, estimator validity, causal efficacy, automatic prose compilation and complete
  warranted protocol generation. No single numerical quality score replaces those distinctions.
