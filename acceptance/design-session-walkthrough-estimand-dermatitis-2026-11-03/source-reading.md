The tralokinumab / Atopic Dermatitis / Phase2 / COMPLETED query returned four IDs; seed 3061570227477035330 selected NCT05388760, NCT02347176 and NCT04556461 without replacement. The complete four-study pool was acquired. The selected set remains clinically heterogeneous; no sample was replaced to obtain a complete estimand.

## NCT05388760

Whole identification, status, description, design, eligibility, arms/interventions and outcomes units read. Results, flow and baseline are not posted in this snapshot. Structured age is 6–11 Years; prose states 6 to less than 12 at baseline. Primary endpoint is PK trough concentration at week16, with other PK windows tied to Q2W/Q4W regimens. Secondary EASI, SCORAD and POEM changes are measured to week68. Description allows medically necessary rescue treatment at the trial doctor's discretion. It does not state, in these retained units, what post-rescue response is intended to mean or how missing assessments enter that clinical target. Rescue permission is not an estimand strategy.

## NCT02347176

Whole selected protocol units, complete participant-flow and baseline units, and complete efficacy outcome results at indices0,1,6,7,8,9 read. Randomized placebo plus three dose groups, actual204 participants, structured18–75 Years. Flow says95 screenfailures and204 randomized and treated; ITT is explicitly defined as all randomized AND treated grouped by assignment. Flow starts51/50/51/52 and completes39/40/43/48, with lost-to-follow-up, subject withdrawal and other reasons. Completion is not identical to an available week12 measurement.

The primary EASI change description excludes data from participants who took prohibited medications. EASI50 (outcome6) and SCORAD50 (outcome8) add LOCF. EASI50 is at least50% reduction from baseline at week12; the outcome denominator rows still list51/50/51/52. These reported denominators do not reveal individual missingness, rescue use, or the exact post-event estimand. No numerical claim about exclusions can be derived from those equal denominators. Prohibited medication is not equated with rescue medication. LOCF is an explicitly reported analysis technique, not a supplied proof that a clinical target is hypothetical or that a carried-forward observation is an observed week12 value.

## Encoding boundary

A clinical estimand requires choices beyond these retained fragments. Any finite definition refs, event relevance rule, required collection window/mode or actual-input scenario introduced for mechanism acceptance will be explicitly an assistant proposal or invented sensitivity, not a registry fact and not clinician-confirmed. Strategy labels alone must supply no requirement, statistical method or structural nonexistence rule. No actual patient data or estimation will be manufactured.
