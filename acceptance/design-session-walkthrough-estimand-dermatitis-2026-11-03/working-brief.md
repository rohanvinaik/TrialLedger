## Executed consequences

The final request/result pairs preserve all revisions. In the explicit hypothetical sensitivity, required+stop gives a collection mismatch and missing relevant observation. Revising the plan alone aligns collection but leaves the missing observation. Revising actual observed availability aligns all supplied checks. An alternative relevance rule makes the unavailable observation not required without claiming it exists. Changing only the strategy label derives no rule. Proposed support is visibly refused even when both aggregate labels are unresolved.

The two interpretation frames are unresolved versus compatible; both leave clinical appropriateness undetermined. No estimator rule or estimation was supplied or inferred. Source age results remain known independently: the two adult records entail age≥18, the pediatric record refutes it. These are checks of the records' own age restrictions, not automatic choices for the new draft.
