# Clinical information handoffs: implementation and verification

This repair closes the audited evidence-access and reasoning-explanation gaps. It preserves the distinction
between a retained source value, an accepted interpretation, a default, a proposal and a new design choice.
It does not complete the separately planned automated protocol writer or certify clinical effectiveness.

The [five clinical conversations](clinical-handoffs-2026-11-03/README.md) are model-operated simulations,
not actual clinician confirmations. They cover Phase 1 allergy, Phase 2 migraine, Phase 3 NSCLC, Phase 4
hypertension and observational pulmonary fibrosis. Each includes a consequential revision, independent
facts that should survive it, and an explicit list of unresolved clinical questions.

## Delivery checks

The delivery gate is `scripts/ci_local.sh`: locked environment, Ruff, formatting, the complete non-live
pytest suite and advisory Ty. After adoption of the exact generated tests, its test phase passed **6,060
tests**, with ten live tests deselected; Ruff, formatting and Ty also passed. The separate live run passed
all ten. The regular gate is rerun on the final delivery tree before commit; its tree stamp and final
delivery record are outside the committed tree. Prior measurements are not presented as a test of later
artifact changes.

The full-project deep mode additionally revisits 230 historically tagged pinned decisions. This run was
deliberately stopped after reaching unchanged `cells.py` targets whose reports requested reconvergence;
it exited 143 and is **incomplete**, not a project-wide pass. Three complete target reports and the start
of a fourth are retained in the [partial audit report](../openspec/changes/repair-clinical-information-handoffs/evidence/historical-deep-audit-incomplete.txt).
That interrupted run's regular test phase is the 6,060-test measurement above. Its whole log is retained
at `/tmp/trialledger-handoff-delivery-deep-gate.log`. No historical target was rewritten or relabelled to
clear that audit. The repair's current scoped packets remain the proof evidence in the table above.
The accepted validator limit and older accepted pin limits remain visible. The broader default-conflict
strata and complete automated writer remain separately unfinished.

Only complete selected clinical request/result pairs, readable briefs and receipts are committed. Raw
response archives, duplicate wire captures, intermediate attempts and report workbooks remain in retained
run folders named by the case receipts. A new registry fetch cannot guarantee historical byte recovery.
