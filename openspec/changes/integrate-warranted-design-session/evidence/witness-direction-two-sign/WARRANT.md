# Direction-aware locality of an eligibility witness

Scope: typed candidate criteria already admitted by the existing entailment owner, one feasible participant profile, and a separately admitted positive witness count/eligibility bridge. All ECOG/marker teaching values are invented; no source translation is inferred here.

Let K be the conjunction of the candidate's admitted necessary constraints, U a conjunction of additional withheld criteria, and P the observed profile. Actual REFUTED for an accepted profile establishes that K AND P is unsatisfiable. Therefore K AND U AND P remains unsatisfiable for every additional U. The elimination depends on the admitted deciding proof and on the observation/bridge support, not on U. This does not survive a revision that retracts K or changes its scope/unit/time; that is a new request requiring recomputation.

A rejected witness asks whether P AND NOT(K AND U) has a completion. U could itself explain rejection even when P implies K. Therefore withheld criteria remain blocking in that direction. A rejected ECOG2 profile does not establish universal exclusion of ECOG2.

The implementation admits only four known withholding-prefix families for accepted + actual REFUTED: proposed_refused:, unresolved_assertion:, unresolved_rule:, conflicting:. Unknown causes, open binding/scope, opaque profiles, unsupported queries and computation limits remain blockers. Observation support and intrinsic-profile checks retain their existing owners and gates. Raw decision blockers, proof and status are retained unchanged; the reduction alone changes. Both production callers pass the actual sign explicitly. The three-argument compatibility default is accepted.

MUST: accepted ECOG4 contradicts an admitted ECOG<=3 requirement even beside a proposed marker requirement. Its deciding proof is the known ECOG criterion. MUST NOT: a rejected ECOG2 profile with that withheld marker requirement eliminate the candidate. MUST NOT: absent/proposed/unknown witnesses, wrong scope, intrinsic inconsistency, opaque input, resource limits or future unknown blockers eliminate a candidate.

Corpus statement rates remain descriptive and do not use this witness reducer. A candidate-family consequence retains the actual elimination standing, including an interpolated observation bridge when it is necessary. No result claims the declared candidates exhaust real protocols or that the exact registry eligibility was recovered.
