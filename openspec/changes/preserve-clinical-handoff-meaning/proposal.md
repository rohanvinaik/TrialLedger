# Proposal

## Why

The Phase 2 ulcerative-colitis public workflow computed useful permissions and competing readings that its main review surfaces could not faithfully deliver. A related source-reading artifact breaks export, query-level explanations lose their option identity, and revision provenance can retain stale warrants for changed content.

## What Changes

- Export valid related source-assertion analyses without assuming a managed-session analysis schema.
- Carry explicit permissions, gates, standing and references into the main decision workbook and Markdown beside independent remaining requirements.
- Keep open-item explanations attached to their represented option and query/complement distinction.
- Reconcile changed user-choice assertion references with their new adoption origin while preserving independent original/source references and immutable prior artifacts.
- Keep identifying decision/option columns visible during horizontal review.
- Add direct positive/MUST-NOT tests, scoped Detective two-sign receipts, real CLI/MCP replay and fresh disease/phase workflow verification.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `symbolic-trial-design`: operation-aware review composition, permission-conserving handoffs, option-scoped explanations and content-consistent revision adoption provenance.

## Impact

Layer-two delivery only: clinical_view, clinical_export and reasoning_delivery, plus their tests, operator documentation and acceptance receipts. No new clinical text interpretation, source mutation, solver outcome rule, dependency or layer-one reporting change. The separate large-source-reference architecture and complete protocol writer are not part of these four audit repairs.
