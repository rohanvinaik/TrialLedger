# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Review composition respects result ownership
Review export SHALL compose valid related reasoning artifacts according to their owning operation, preserving separate conditional findings and canonical references without assigning invented managed-analysis identities.

#### Scenario: Source-bound rival readings accompany a session
- **WHEN** a valid source-assertion result is included with a completed managed session
- **THEN** CLI and MCP export succeed, retain its separate findings and proposed warrants, and do not attach a reading to an unrelated decision

### Requirement: Primary decisions retain permission channels
The main decision workbook and readable list SHALL show returned permissions with applicability, scope, standing and provenance beside independent requirements and open checks. A permission SHALL NOT be displayed as a requirement or complete eligibility finding.

#### Scenario: Rescreening is permitted conditionally
- **WHEN** a draft permits rescreening after treatment and separately excludes current pathogen positivity
- **THEN** the main decision output shows the conditional permission and outstanding positivity check without requiring a rescreening attempt or asserting eligibility

#### Scenario: No relevant permission is returned
- **WHEN** no accepted permission concerns a query
- **THEN** the output does not manufacture permission or infer prohibition

### Requirement: Review explanations retain option identity
Decision-level open items SHALL identify their represented option and distinguish an exact proposition's feasible complement from alternative design options. Identifying columns SHALL remain visible during horizontal review of the decision and option sheets.

#### Scenario: Two weeks fits but one week does not
- **WHEN** a two-week minimum refutes a one-week profile and permits an exact two-week profile
- **THEN** the settled decision does not imply that both declared profiles fit, while the exact-value query can remain allowed but not required

### Requirement: Revision adoption references match changed content
A content-changing assertion edit attributed to a new user choice SHALL rebind references identifying the prior adoption exchange to the new exchange, preserve independent source references and prior artifacts, and leave epistemic standing unchanged.

#### Scenario: A threshold choice is revised
- **WHEN** the numeric threshold changes with a new user-choice origin
- **THEN** its old adoption references identify the new choice, independent citations remain intact, and before/after evidence remains inspectable

#### Scenario: A reference is independent of adoption
- **WHEN** a source citation or different rule warrant does not equal the prior user-choice reference
- **THEN** revision does not relabel that reference as the new human exchange or promote its status
