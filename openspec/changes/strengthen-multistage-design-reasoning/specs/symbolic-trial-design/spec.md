## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Public typed preparation removes repetitive encoding work
Public CLI/MCP SHALL expand short explicit typed criteria and questions/options into runnable canonical requests, returning applied defaults and origins. Preparation SHALL remain inspection, preserve logical structure and clinical qualifiers, and default unconfirmed standing to proposed. It SHALL NOT interpret prose or authenticate confirmation.

#### Scenario: An LLM prepares a multistage design without Python helpers
- **WHEN** it supplies typed criteria, scopes and option profiles through the discovered preparation contract
- **THEN** it receives an inspectable runnable request and origin/default ledgers using the same downstream validators and solver

#### Scenario: A prepared expansion is larger than a conversational reply
- **WHEN** a successful preparation is retained and run by its artifact identity
- **THEN** the exact expansion executes without resending it, preserving its construction ledger separately from clinical premises and later edits

### Requirement: Residual changes do not impersonate verdict changes
Revision SHALL compare remaining requirements independently of the verdict, consistently for option analyses and parent decisions. Both changed requirements and actual changed verdicts SHALL remain visible with their own before/after values and warrants.

#### Scenario: ECOG widening changes another profile's outstanding requirements
- **WHEN** an allowed CNS profile stays allowed while its criteria-wide ECOG condition widens
- **THEN** the change report says remaining requirements changed with verdict unchanged, rather than a changed CNS verdict

### Requirement: Scope uncertainty follows the question and proof
Open-scope blockers SHALL accompany the query component or actual deciding proof, rather than unrelated criteria. Global contradiction and computation limits SHALL remain visible. Whole-eligibility negative witnesses SHALL consider every applicable restriction.

#### Scenario: A later-stage responder rule is unrelated to entry exclusion
- **WHEN** a universal exclusion refutes an entry profile and another criterion has an open later-stage cohort
- **THEN** the exclusion remains refuted with its warrant and no unrelated open-scope blocker

#### Scenario: Scope genuinely changes the answer
- **WHEN** the only supporting rule applies to an unspecified cohort
- **THEN** its open scope remains unresolved rather than assumed to be trial entry

#### Scenario: A draft never addresses an option
- **WHEN** a new-trial question has only unrepresented constraints
- **THEN** it is a missing design question rather than a historical evidence gap or proof that both alternatives are feasible

### Requirement: Finite tables enforce declared forbidden actions
Finite tables SHALL accept optional forbidden-action conditions, check them against feasible cases and retain violations independently of coverage. They SHALL expose their declared variables and bounded case illustrations without claiming undeclared safety properties.

#### Scenario: A covered table escalates under a forbidden safety condition
- **WHEN** a feasible case selects escalation and satisfies the declared escalation prohibition
- **THEN** the table reports a conflict, the prohibition and a bounded witness without deleting that case

#### Scenario: No prohibition is declared
- **WHEN** a table is covered and conflict-free
- **THEN** it reports only structural completeness over declared domains, not clinical safety

#### Scenario: A small finite table uses a large per-call ceiling
- **WHEN** its represented domain and every guard/prohibition threshold establish smaller bounded work
- **THEN** session reservation uses that conservative represented work without weakening execution or illustration limits

### Requirement: Cross-analysis definitions use explicit relations
Sessions SHALL check explicitly attributed typed meanings bound to exact analysis addresses for declared implication, equivalence or disjointness. They SHALL preserve scope, standing, source values, relation and deciding results. Missing or proposed meaning SHALL NOT become a known cross-operation constraint.

#### Scenario: An early safety trigger differs from a later event definition
- **WHEN** the person declares that the later event must imply the earlier trigger
- **THEN** implication is checked without demanding equality of the thresholds

#### Scenario: Two definitions are intended to be identical
- **WHEN** an accepted equivalence declaration has a feasible counterexample
- **THEN** the session exposes the failed direction and warrants as a conflict relative to that declaration

### Requirement: Included analyses reach the readable handoff
Clinical exports SHALL summarize included table, population-stage and estimand results beside the decision list, preserving actual values, denominator scopes, standing, assumptions and exact result addresses. Routine pairwise non-overlap details SHALL remain accessible without overwhelming the summary.

#### Scenario: A withdrawal-stage population bound is included
- **WHEN** the analysis computes a bounded intersection under declared assumptions
- **THEN** Markdown and workbook summaries retain that interval, its population and assumptions rather than only a detailed Findings dump

### Requirement: Retained source handles preserve complete reasoning evidence
Public reasoning transport SHALL resolve explicit source handles by served report, NCT identity and content digest. Canonical artifacts SHALL retain the complete hydrated study without promoting interpretations or relying on a subsequently mutable handle. Invalid or changed references SHALL refuse before reasoning.

#### Scenario: An MCP operator attaches a comparator quote
- **WHEN** exact retained source text is retrieved and its digest-bound handle is supplied in an accepted source slot
- **THEN** source-backed reasoning, revision and export preserve the complete study and quote address
