# Proposal

## Why

The obesity walkthrough exposes consequences the existing solver proves but its explainer does not
discover, and a handoff that separates those consequences from their evidence and represented questions.
Layer two should expose warranted information without giving caller wording or comparator context
the standing of a computed clinical assertion.

## What Changes

- Discover bounded residual requirements across typed equivalent rule forms, with existing solver
  warrants, compound alternatives, feasibility gates, weakest support and conservative work accounting.
- Bind optional decision context to exact retained source anchors; keep copied evidence, proposed
  readings, caller-described gaps, user choices and deciding premises separate.
- Share a compositional typed-query display and decision view across conversation, bounded retrieval,
  Markdown and spreadsheets. Caller questions/labels remain visibly attributed.
- Separate question changes, verdict/content changes, deciding-warrant changes and origin changes.
- Bound compact replies, retain full canonical artifacts and offer a self-contained decision access part
  with explicit continuation instead of repeated object-directory navigation.
- Expose the actual estimand strategy vocabulary and structured display queries without rewriting
  original warrants. Clarify the existing atomic export-folder contract.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None; these improve the existing symbolic reasoning and handoff capability.

### Modified Capabilities

- `symbolic-trial-design`: residual explanations, anchored decision context, clinical presentation,
  revision semantics, bounded artifact access and executable discovery.

## Impact

Explanation planner and solver-work reservation; session state and evidence anchors; clinical projection
and comparison; shared clinical display, export and compact delivery; CLI/MCP discovery/access; authored
tests, Detective receipts, real clinical walkthroughs and operator documentation. Legacy requests remain
accepted. No automatic clinical prose compiler, consent authentication, new medical recommendation,
sample-size engine or complete protocol writer is introduced.
