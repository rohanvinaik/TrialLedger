# Automated clinical trial writing

## Why

The clinical reference record is the warranted substrate for the project's main symbolic design thesis:
construct a new trial from a brief, what follows from its constraints, and what comparable trials establish.
Correct partial logic already has utility. Missing compilation and orchestration must be completed without
replacing the design with word matching or misrepresenting component results as a finished protocol.

## What changes

Consolidate the existing coherence, scoped assertion, corpus geometry, surprise, evidence-source and design
plans into one layer-two design. Preserve executable reasoning components, make their input contracts and
availability clear, and complete the source-to-protocol pipeline along the existing design. Keep every
inference, default, proposal and choice out of ordinary layer-one reporting.

## Capabilities

- `symbolic-trial-design`: scoped evidence and entailment, domain constraints, corpus geometry and
  significance, gap-directed evidence, admissible recommendations, user closure and warranted protocol output.

## Impact

The design governs the separate `TrialLedger.layer2` package and its agent entry point. The current partial
operations are not a public promise of a complete new protocol. Layer-one completion does not depend on
finishing this change. Superseded active changes are replaced by this consolidated contract, not accumulated
as additional requirements.
