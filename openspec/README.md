# TrialLedger contract

The authoritative capabilities are [registry reporting](specs/registry-reporting/spec.md) and
[symbolic trial design](specs/symbolic-trial-design/spec.md). The first is the source-faithful reference
record and workbook. The second is the complete automated trial-writing design, with correct partial
operations available before the whole pipeline is integrated.

[The theory](../docs/THEORY_OF_THE_CASE.md) records epistemology and source authority.
[The delivery boundary](MVP_PATH.md) separates reporting from the designer. The two active changes are
[reporting repairs](changes/refine-landscape-record/design.md) and
[the consolidated designer](changes/add-design-synthesis/design.md). Task completion describes verified
implementation, not whether the design has been written.

The initial workbook change in `changes/archive/` is historical evidence, not another active instruction.

Future standards interoperability is proposed in the [ICH M11 / CDISC USDM roadmap](../docs/M11_USDM_ROADMAP.md)
and [OpenSpec change](changes/add-m11-usdm-interoperability/proposal.md). Its planning artifacts and GitHub
tracking describe future delivery; they do not claim a working adapter or current standards conformance.

Superseded agent-written changes were removed during this reset; Git retains their history. They must not
be reintroduced as reporting prerequisites.
