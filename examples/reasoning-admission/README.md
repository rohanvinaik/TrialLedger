# An accepted fact beside a pending reading

All premises here are invented teaching inputs. The scale bridge is an invented relation between two
particular propositions, not evidence that WHO PS and ECOG scales are universally equivalent.

Ask: “How many comparison trials require adults and exclude brain metastases? Can we compare their PS
limits, and is the treatment setting established?”

- In `mixed-comparison`, both age readings are accepted; one brain-metastasis reading is pending. Age has
  two admitted statements out of two. The exclusion has one admitted and one pending statement, preserving
  the [0.5,1] full-criteria range. The pending PS field prevents a near-miss claim. Its pending setting still
  preserves independently known class A membership, so it does not become a possible class B occupant.
  One unconfirmed expectation observation is withheld, so the support-two rarity abstains.
- In `coordinate-standing`, EGFR selection is known positive. Prior advanced lines =0 is interpolated,
  not known; its setting consequence stays in the weaker layer. A proposed negative-EGFR reading remains
  visible rather than manufacturing a known conflict.
- In `unbound-scale`, the solver cannot transfer a same-predicate measurement from ECOG units to WHO units.
  Its diagnostic names the mismatch and retains both identities. It never supplies equivalence.
- In `declared-proposition-bridge`, an explicitly invented equivalence supports the query conditionally.
  The conclusion remains interpolated, naming the bridge in its proof. Removing or proposing that bridge
  withdraws the consequence.
